1. CDS Pattern — What the PYQs Tell You

The UPSC archive confirms separate CDS-I/CDS-II General Knowledge papers for the period; the pattern used here is a synthesis of the recurring question style across the 2021–2025 papers rather than a reproduction of PYQs.

🔴 VERY HIGH Country → Geography

Country in news → capital, currency, neighbours, landlocked status, sea/ocean, strait/gulf/canal. This is the highest-value conversion for CDS.

🔴 VERY HIGH International organisations

HQ, membership, India’s status, chair/presidency, new member and summit venue are recurring static-current combinations.

🟠 HIGH Awards & honours

Person → award → country → highest/not highest → date → institution. Do not blindly label every order as a “highest civilian award”.

🟠 HIGH Defence exercises

Exercise → countries → service → location → purpose. CDS often rewards exact pairing and geography.

PYQ takeaway: Do not read current affairs as prose. Build fact chains: event → country → border → capital → currency → maritime feature → grouping → likely MCQ.
