Most Important for UPSC

Study these first

  1. 72nd National Film Awards: award edition is 72nd, announced in 2026, but awards films of 2024.
  2. Article 370 → Best Feature Film; Kartik Aaryan + Mammootty → Best Actor; Yami Gautam → Best Actress.
  3. Boong became a major 2026 UPSC linkage point: it won the BAFTA Children's & Family Film award and was directly tested in CSE Prelims 2026.
  4. 98th Oscars: One Battle after Another → Best Picture; Sentimental Value → International Feature Film for Norway.
  5. 79th Cannes: FJORD → Palme d'Or.
  6. 76th Berlin / 83rd Venice: know Golden Bear, Golden Lion and Palme d'Or.
  7. 2026 cross-award pattern: One Battle after Another and Hamnet recur across major Western awards—ideal for matching questions.
PYQ-Derived Trend

What UPSC is actually rewarding

  • CSE Prelims: increasingly uses statement combinations built around a current cultural development rather than asking only “Who won?”.
  • CDS: comparatively more likely to ask direct factual matching—winner ↔ award, film ↔ director, country ↔ international category.
  • Highest-yield pattern: current winner plus one static attribute: category, country, institution, language, “first”, or chronology.
  • 2026 proof: the CSE Prelims question on Boong combined award, director and “first Indian film” claims.
What NOT to Memorise

Low-return preparation

  • Complete Filmfare/IIFA winner lists.
  • Technical categories unless linked to an exceptional record or major Indian connection.
  • Long lists of nominees.
  • Obscure regional/private awards without UPSC-level news relevance.
  • Every winner at every international festival.

UPSC value = Current relevance × Prominence × Matchability.

12 High-Yield Practice Questions

Q1 With reference to the 72nd National Film Awards, consider the following statements: 1. The awards announced in 2026 recognised films of 2024. 2. Article 370 won the Best Feature Film award. 3. Mammootty and Kartik Aaryan shared the Best Actor in a Leading Role award. Which are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (d) — All three statements are correct.

Source: PIB.

Q2 Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Yami Gautam — Best Actress for Article 370
(b) Randeep Hooda — Best Direction for Amaran
(c) Mammootty — Best Actor for Chandu Champion
(d) Rajkumar Periasamy — Best Popular Film for Kalki 2898 AD

Answer: (a)

Source: PIB.
